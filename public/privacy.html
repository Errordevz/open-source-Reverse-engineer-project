Privacy policy

Openfy is a static, local-first music collection notebook. There is no Openfy-operated backend, analytics service, advertising network, or telemetry.

Information stored on this device

  • Your notes, personal tags, and selected track references are stored in this browser's local storage.
  • The optional Spotify access token and temporary OAuth state are stored in session storage.
  • Your Spotify Client ID is stored locally in this browser so you do not need to re-enter it each time.

Spotify connection

If you choose to connect, your browser communicates directly with Spotify's official authorization and Web API endpoints. Openfy requests only basic profile information, private playlist read access, and collaborative playlist read access. Openfy does not send your notes or saved collection to Spotify.

Spotify controls its own data practices and services. Review Spotify's privacy information and Developer Policy before connecting.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Spotify from Settings. Disconnecting clears the current session token and deletes the locally saved Spotify-linked track metadata and associated notes from this browser. You can also clear your local collection separately. Removing browser storage or site data also deletes locally stored information.

Third-party services

Spotify is an independent third-party service. Openfy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved by Spotify. Spotify is a trademark of its respective owner.

Zero monetization: Openfy contains no ads, subscriptions, paid tiers, donations, sponsorships, affiliate links, or in-app purchases.
