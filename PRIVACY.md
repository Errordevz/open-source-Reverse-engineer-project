# Privacy

Openfy is a static, local-first web application. There is no Openfy server.

- Your saved collection and personal notes are stored in this browser's local storage.
- OAuth access tokens are stored in session storage and are removed when you disconnect or when the session expires.
- When you use Spotify search or playlist listing, your browser communicates directly with Spotify's official API.
- Openfy does not send your notes, saved collection, or Spotify credentials to an Openfy-operated service.
- Spotify's own privacy policy and developer terms apply to your use of Spotify services.

Disconnecting Spotify removes the current session token and deletes Spotify-linked track metadata and associated notes from this browser. You can also clear local collection data in Settings at any time.
